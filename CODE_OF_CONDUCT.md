# Code of conduct

Phone Backup is a small project, and we want everyone who takes part to feel welcome: people reporting a bug, asking a question, suggesting an idea or sending code.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not technical, and every question is fair.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasons.
- Accept feedback on your work gracefully, and give it the same way.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal data, and don't ask people to share their backups, contacts or messages.

## What is not okay

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Discrimination of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information without their clear permission
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a public project

## Where it applies

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and comments, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately to the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mitunmanav), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment. Every report is read and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules, starting with a warning where that's enough.
