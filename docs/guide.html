Your first backup

About 5 minutes, plus the time to copy.

  1. Download the app

    Get the file from the download page and double-click it. Nothing to install. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. The app is not code-signed, which is why Windows asks.

  2. Connect your phone

    Plug it in with a USB cable and unlock it. For photos and files, choose File transfer in the phone's USB notification. For apps, contacts and messages, use USB debugging. All three ways are below.

  3. Pick what to copy

    On Back up, your phone appears with a tile for each kind of file, with its size. Turn tiles on or off, or press Choose folders to pick single folders such as only your camera photos.

  4. Press Back up now

    Keep the phone plugged in and unlocked. You can pause and carry on later; Stop keeps what has already been copied.

  5. Open your backup

    It is a normal folder named after your phone. report.txt lists what was copied and anything that failed. Next time, only new or changed files are copied.

Three ways to connect

Use whichever suits you. The app shows what to tap on your phone's brand.

File transfer

Plug in, unlock, tap the USB notification and choose File transfer. Copies photos, videos and files. No setup.

USB debugging

Adds apps, contacts, calls, messages and the calendar. In Settings, open About phone and tap Build number 7 times, then turn on USB debugging in Developer options. In the app, click Get adb to fetch Google's free tool, and tap Allow on the phone.

Wi-Fi

No cable after setup. On the phone, open Developer options, then Wireless debugging, tap Pair device with QR code and scan the code the app shows.

Put a backup back on a phone

Works with any Android phone, not only the one you backed up.

  1. Choose a backup

    Open Restore and pick the backup. Its size and date are shown.

  2. Choose what comes back

    Everything, or press Choose files to pick from a list with previews. Large files, duplicates and old files are marked, so they are easy to leave out. Press Version to go back to the backup as it was on an earlier day.

  3. Press Restore now

    Plug in the phone with USB debugging on. Files the phone already has are skipped, never replaced. Apps come back without their logins and data.

Move to a new phone

Copy one phone straight to another. Both need USB debugging.

  1. Connect both phones

    Open Transfer. Check which phone is Copy from and which is Copy to; press Swap if they are the wrong way round.

  2. Press Check transfer

    The app backs up the first phone and shows what will change. Nothing changes on either phone yet.

  3. Press Transfer now

    Keep both phones plugged in and unlocked until it finishes.

Use a backup without a phone

Everything here works on the PC alone.

Read it

On Tools, read messages, contacts, calls and the calendar, with search.

Export it

On Restore, Export saves contacts, calls and messages as spreadsheets and pages you can print, the calendar for Google Calendar or Outlook, and photos sorted by year and month.

Check it

Verify on Restore checks that no file has changed since it was saved.
